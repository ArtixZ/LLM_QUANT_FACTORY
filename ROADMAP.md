# AutoAlpha Roadmap

The roadmap prioritizes research integrity and independent return discovery over raw iteration
count. Items are directional; an issue and evidence contract should precede major implementation.

## 1. Factor knowledge management

- Hierarchical mechanism ontology with explicit aliases and ownership.
- Parameter-family folding and canonical expression fingerprints.
- Annual/regime decay profiles and structured failure reasons.
- Queryable evidence lineage from data snapshot to strategy version.
- Marginal-contribution and substitutability heatmaps.
- Compact views that fold homogeneous factors while preserving every asset.

## 2. Multi-model and multi-thread collaboration

- Structured role-to-role handoffs instead of shared free-form chat.
- Independent model diversity and disagreement scoring.
- Per-role memory, context budgets and evidence citations.
- Asynchronous research branches with deterministic merge criteria.
- Cost, latency and provider-failure controls.
- Reproducible deliberation artifacts without exposing hidden-test metrics.

## 3. Homogeneity and false novelty

- AST, unit, timing, field and parameter signatures.
- Signal and return behavior sketches over common evaluation windows.
- Online clustering and novelty budgets by mechanism.
- Residual target discovery relative to the active portfolio.
- Rewards based on stable marginal contribution, not standalone Sharpe alone.
- Counterfactual tests that detect cosmetic transformations and parameter mining.

## 4. PIT data and execution realism

- Historical listing, delisting, halt/LULD and exchange eligibility.
- Effective-dated open-time tradability and corporate-action knowledge.
- Corporate-action knowledge and revision timestamps.
- Point-in-time free-float capitalization and industry membership.
- Whole shares, cash, settlement, SEC/FINRA fees, impact, capacity and rejected-order replay.
- Independent event-ledger/vector reconciliation fixtures.

## 5. Strategy lifecycle

- Explicit entry, exit, rebalance and fallback rules.
- Immutable strategy specifications and evidence diffs.
- Shadow, paper and controlled production stages.
- Live decay, crowding, turnover, capacity and data-drift monitors.
- Rollback, retirement and post-mortem workflows.

## 6. Open research ecosystem

- Provider-neutral data adapters and market contracts.
- Additional market protocol packs beyond the current US-equity protocol.
- Small synthetic datasets for end-to-end tutorials.
- Reproducible benchmark suites for factor deduplication and agent collaboration.
- Import/export schemas for external factor libraries and portfolio optimizers.

## Near-term priorities

1. Publish the factor-knowledge and strategy-spec schemas.
2. Add deterministic synthetic end-to-end fixtures.
3. Establish behavior-clustering benchmarks.
4. Separate LLM collaboration transport from research policy.
5. Complete PIT execution-data adapters before any production claim.
