# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous and welcoming project. Participants should
be able to discuss models, statistics, failures and competing designs without personal hostility
or pressure to overstate results.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-led.
- Welcome questions and explain assumptions.
- Credit prior work and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Treat failed experiments as useful evidence.
- Protect private data, credentials and unpublished research.
- Separate criticism of an idea from criticism of a person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of
private information, market manipulation, fabricated evidence and repeated bad-faith disruption
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@jiangjingzhe.com`. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, restrict participation or ban contributors when necessary. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical.
